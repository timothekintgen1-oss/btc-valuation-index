Indicators · current Z-score (higher = more expensive)
Each indicator is oriented so that a higher reading means a more expensive market (a high 52-week Sharpe ratio is treated as overheated), then converted to a rolling 6-year z-score using only past data. The composite is the equal-weight average of available indicators, re-standardised the same way. BEAM is approximated as price / 200-week moving average; Thermocap uses cumulative miner issuance (fees excluded). BGeometrics series start in 2022 and are published with a 7-day delay; their last value is carried forward up to 10 days.
