Each indicator is oriented so that a higher reading means a more expensive market (a high 52-week Sharpe ratio is treated as overheated), then converted to a rolling 6-year z-score using only past data. The composite is the equal-weight average of available indicators, re-standardised the same way. BEAM is approximated as price / 200-week moving average; Thermocap uses cumulative miner issuance (fees excluded). BGeometrics series start in 2022 and are published with a 7-day delay; their last value is carried forward up to 10 days.

INDICATOR HEATMAP · Z-SCORE BY INDICATOR
SDCA backtest · value ÷ amount invested, weekly buys weighted by the Z-score
Every 7 days from 1 January of the chosen year, at that day's close. DCA: buy $100. SDCA: buy $100 × (1 − Z), capped at 3× and never below 0 — $300 at Z ≤ −2, $100 at Z = 0, nothing from Z = 1. SDCA + take profit: same buys, and sell 5% of the BTC held each week while Z ≥ 2 (10% while Z ≥ 3); proceeds stay in cash. Value = BTC × latest price + cash. The composite only uses past data each day, except the power-law fit behind one indicator; fees and taxes ignored. Not investment advice.
