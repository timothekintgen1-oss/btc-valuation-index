Three models, one market · risk 0–100 % (low = cheap)
BVI compares each date with the six previous years, using only past data: robust, but it under-reads the shrinking cycle tops of 2021 and 2025. SDCA model is your teacher's composite, reproduced exactly (price position in the RAQQR quantile model + cost-basis P/L + on-chain risk composite): it flags those tops, partly because its price coefficients were fitted on the whole 2010–2026 history. The causal twin swaps that price pillar for a past-only power law, to show what survives without hindsight. Consensus averages BVI and SDCA.
COMPOSITE RISK
BVI risk = normal CDF of the composite Z-score; SDCA risk = 50 − Z/3 × 50 on its ±3 scale. Low risk = cheaper / more buyable; high risk = expensive / less buyable. Valuation risk, not an allocation.

ASYMMETRIC TAIL CURVATURE · QUANTILE RAINBOW
The price model of the SDCA app (RAQQR, Cowen 2026): the 1 % to 99 % quantiles of log price are quadratic curves in log time, with more curvature in the upper tail — tops get flatter each cycle. Bands use the published full-sample coefficients, so past bands were drawn with hindsight; after the last close the curves are only extended, not forecast.

Optimizer · walk-forward
Tests 1,050 simple curves (buy rate, where buying stops, where selling starts, how hard it sells) on the window before the split date, keeps the best one, then shows how it did after the split, on data it never saw. A curve that only wins before the split is fitted to the past.
 
Weekly strategies on the BVI · value ÷ amount invested
Every 7 days from 1 January of the chosen year, at that day's close. DCA: buy $100. SDCA: buy $100 × (1 − Z), capped at 3× and never below 0 — $300 at Z ≤ −2, $100 at Z = 0, nothing from Z = 1. SDCA + take profit: same buys, and sell 5 % of the BTC held each week while Z ≥ 2 (10 % while Z ≥ 3). Risk zones: buy $200 in the buy zone (risk < 25 %), $100 when accumulating (25–50 %), nothing in the trim zone (50–75 %), and sell 2 % of the BTC held each week in the sell zone (≥ 75 %). Value = BTC × latest price + cash.

Method

Orientation. Each indicator is oriented so that a high reading means an expensive market (a high 52-week Sharpe ratio is treated as overheated).


Standardisation. Each one becomes a rolling 6-year z-score using only past data; the power-law fit is refitted every day on past prices.


Composite. Equal-weight mean of the selected indicators, re-standardised the same way. Weights are not optimised on purpose: that would fit the index to the past.


New in this version. Three on-chain series from the SDCA app (ChartInspect cost-basis P/L, on-chain risk composite, realized P/L Sharpe) with history back to 2010–2011, and the RAQQR quantile model. The cost-basis P/L and the on-chain composite join the default composite; RAQQR and the realized P/L Sharpe are optional because they are rescaled with the whole history.


Approximations. BEAM = price / 200-week moving average; Thermocap uses cumulative miner issuance (fees excluded). BGeometrics series start in 2022 and are published with a 7-day delay.
